How I Plan a Poster Before Opening Photoshop

15 September 2026 · 5 min read

A good poster usually starts before Photoshop. When the idea is clear, the design becomes easier to build, edit and finish. My first step is to decide what the viewer should notice first.

1. Start with one main message

Write the main message in one short sentence. If the viewer can understand the purpose quickly, the poster has a stronger foundation.

2. Choose the visual direction

Before adding effects, decide on the mood, type style, image direction and basic colour approach. A small reference board can save a lot of time later.

3. Build a simple hierarchy

Give the headline the strongest visual weight. Then place supporting information such as a date, offer or call to action. Good hierarchy makes a poster easier to scan.

4. Leave space

Empty space is not wasted space. It gives important elements room to breathe and helps the viewer understand the composition.

5. Design, then refine

Once the structure works, open Photoshop and start creating. Check the design at a small size as well as full size. If the message still feels clear, the poster is doing its job.

Simple planning does not make a design boring. It gives creativity a stronger direction.
