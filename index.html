About

I'm a self-taught graphic designer and digital marketer, currently building my craft one project at a time. My design education came from practice — CorelDRAW, Photoshop, and hours spent figuring out what looks right and why.

Alongside design, I work on digital marketing and short-form video, editing reels and content in CapCut for social platforms.
